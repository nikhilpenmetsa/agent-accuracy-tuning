# ACME Corporation Employee Handbook

## Welcome to ACME

Welcome to ACME Corporation! This handbook provides essential information about our company policies, benefits, and workplace guidelines.

## Company Overview

ACME Corporation is a leading provider of innovative solutions, founded in 1995. We employ over 5,000 people across 15 locations worldwide.

### Our Mission
To deliver exceptional products and services while fostering a culture of innovation, integrity, and inclusivity.

### Our Values
- Innovation: We embrace creativity and new ideas
- Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency
- Collaboration: We work together to achieve common goals
- Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in everything we do

## Employment Basics

### Work Hours
- Standard work week: Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Lunch break: 1 hour (unpaid)
- Total weekly hours: 40 hours

### Attendance and Punctuality
Employees are expected to arrive on time and maintain regular attendance. If you need to be absent or late, notify your manager at least 2 hours in advance.

### Dress Code
- Business casual attire Monday-Thursday
- Casual Friday (jeans and company t-shirts permitted)
- Client-facing roles require business professional attire

## Compensation

### Payroll
- Pay periods: Bi-weekly (every other Friday)
- Direct deposit is required for all employees
- Pay stubs are available through the employee portal

### Performance Reviews
- Annual performance reviews conducted in December
- Mid-year check-ins in June
- Merit increases based on performance (typically 2-5%)

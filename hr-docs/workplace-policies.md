# ACME Corporation Workplace Policies

## Code of Conduct

All ACME employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
- Act with integrity and honesty in all business dealings
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Protect company assets and confidential information
- Report any violations of policy or law

## Anti-Discrimination and Harassment Policy

ACME is committed to providing a workplace free from discrimination and harassment.

### Protected Classes
We do not discriminate based on:
- Race, color, or national origin
- Religion or creed
- Sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Age (40 and over)
- Disability or medical condition
- Veteran or military status
- Genetic information
- Pregnancy or parental status

### Harassment Prevention
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited, including:
- Verbal harassment (slurs, jokes, insults)
- Physical harassment (unwanted touching, assault)
- Visual harassment (offensive images, gestures)
- Sexual harassment (unwanted advances, requests for favors)

### Reporting Violations
Report any discrimination or harassment to:
1. Your direct manager
2. HR Department (hr@acmecorp.com)
3. Ethics Hotline: 1-800-ETHICS-1 (anonymous reporting available)

All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

## Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility
- Must be employed for at least 6 months
- Role must be suitable for remote work
- Manager approval required
- Must have reliable internet connection and appropriate workspace

### Remote Work Arrangements

**Hybrid Schedule:**
- 2-3 days in office, 2-3 days remote
- Core collaboration days: Tuesday and Wednesday (in-office)

**Full Remote:**
- Available for specific roles with VP approval
- Must be available during core business hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Quarterly in-person meetings required

### Remote Work Expectations
- Maintain regular work hours
- Be responsive during business hours
- Attend all required meetings
- Maintain secure home office setup
- Use company-provided equipment only

## Technology and Equipment

### Company Equipment
- Laptop and necessary peripherals provided
- Equipment remains company property
- Must be returned upon termination
- Report lost or stolen equipment immediately

### Acceptable Use Policy
Company technology may only be used for:
- Business purposes
- Reasonable personal use (email, web browsing)

Prohibited activities:
- Accessing inappropriate or illegal content
- Installing unauthorized software
- Sharing passwords or access credentials
- Using company resources for personal business ventures

### Data Security
- Use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters)
- Enable multi-factor authentication
- Lock devices when unattended
- Do not store sensitive data on personal devices
- Report security incidents immediately to IT

## Social Media Policy

### Personal Social Media
- You may identify yourself as an ACME employee
- Make clear that views are your own, not ACME's
- Do not share confidential company information
- Do not disparage the company, colleagues, or customers
- Be professional and respectful

### Company Social Media
- Only authorized personnel may post on behalf of ACME
- Contact marketing@acmecorp.com for authorization

## Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

### Confidential Information
Employees must protect:
- Trade secrets and proprietary information
- Customer and employee data
- Financial information
- Strategic plans and business strategies
- Unpublished product information

### Intellectual Property
- All work created during employment belongs to ACME
- This includes inventions, designs, code, and creative works
- Employees must sign IP assignment agreement

## Workplace Safety

### General Safety
- Report unsafe conditions immediately
- Follow all safety procedures and protocols
- Use required personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Participate in safety training

### Emergency Procedures
- Know evacuation routes and assembly points
- Report emergencies to security: ext. 911 or 1-800-ACME-SEC
- Do not re-enter building until cleared by authorities

### Workplace Violence Prevention
ACME has zero tolerance for workplace violence, including:
- Physical violence or threats
- Intimidation or coercion
- Weapons on company property (except authorized security)

Report concerns immediately to security or HR.

## Substance Abuse Policy

### Drug-Free Workplace
ACME maintains a drug-free workplace. Prohibited:
- Use, possession, or distribution of illegal drugs
- Being under the influence of alcohol or drugs at work
- Misuse of prescription medications

### Testing
Drug testing may be required:
- Pre-employment
- Post-accident
- Reasonable suspicion
- Random testing for safety-sensitive positions

### Assistance
Employees with substance abuse issues are encouraged to seek help through the EAP before policy violations occur.

## Conflict of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests.

### Examples of Conflicts
- Outside employment with competitors or customers
- Financial interest in competing businesses
- Hiring or supervising family members
- Accepting gifts or favors from vendors (over $100 value)

### Disclosure
Potential conflicts must be disclosed to HR in writing for review and approval.

## Performance and Discipline

### Performance Expectations
- Meet job requirements and goals
- Follow company policies and procedures
- Maintain professional conduct
- Complete required training

### Progressive Discipline
For policy violations or performance issues:
1. Verbal warning (documented)
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning
4. Termination

Serious violations may result in immediate termination.

## Resignation and Termination

### Resignation
- Provide at least 2 weeks written notice
- Return all company property
- Complete exit interview
- Final paycheck issued on last day or next regular pay period

### Termination
- Company may terminate employment at any time (at-will employment)
- Severance may be provided based on circumstances and tenure
- COBRA benefits information provided
- Final paycheck includes unused PTO (where required by law)

## Questions and Concerns

For policy questions or to report violations:
- HR Department: hr@acmecorp.com or 1-800-ACME-HR1
- Ethics Hotline: 1-800-ETHICS-1 (anonymous)
- Open door policy: Speak with any manager or HR representative

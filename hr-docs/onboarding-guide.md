# ACME Corporation New Hire Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to ACME!

Congratulations on joining the ACME team! This guide will help you navigate your first days and weeks at the company.

## Before Your First Day

### Complete Pre-Boarding Tasks
You should have received an email with links to complete:
- I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification
- W-4 Tax Withholding Form
- Direct Deposit Authorization
- Emergency Contact Information
- Background check authorization (if applicable)

### What to Bring
- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport)
- Social Security card or birth certificate
- Voided check for direct deposit (if not completed online)
- Completed I-9 documentation

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will I receive my first paycheck?**
A: Paychecks are issued bi-weekly on Fridays. Your first check will be on the next pay date after you start, covering hours worked.

**Q: How do I request time off?**
A: Submit PTO requests through Workday at least 2 weeks in advance. Your manager will approve or deny based on business needs.

**Q: What if I'm sick and can't come to work?**
A: Notify your manager as soon as possible via email or Slack. Use PTO for sick time.

**Q: Can I work remotely?**
A: After 6 months, you may request a hybrid or remote work arrangement. Discuss with your manager.

**Q: How do I get a parking pass?**
A: Contact facilities@acmecorp.com. Parking is free for all employees.

**Q: What's the dress code?**
A: Business casual Monday-Thursday, casual Friday. Client-facing roles require business professional.

**Q: When can I enroll in benefits?**
A: You have 30 days from your start date to enroll. Benefits become effective the first of the month following your hire date.

**Q: Who do I contact with questions?**
A: Your manager is your first resource. For specific issues, contact HR, IT, or facilities as appropriate.

## 30-60-90 Day Goals

Work with your manager to establish clear goals for your first three months:

### 30-Day Goals (Example)
- Complete all onboarding training
- Understand team structure and key stakeholders
- Learn primary systems and tools
- Complete first small project or task

### 60-Day Goals (Example)
- Take ownership of core responsibilities
- Contribute to team meetings and discussions
- Build relationships across departments
- Demonstrate proficiency in key skills

### 90-Day Goals (Example)
- Operate independently in role
- Meet performance expectations
- Identify opportunities for improvement
- Successfully complete probationary period

## Feedback and Check-ins

### Regular 1:1s with Manager
- Frequency: Weekly or bi-weekly
- Duration: 30-60 minutes
- Topics: Progress, challenges, feedback, development

### 30-Day Check-in
Informal conversation with manager to:
- Review progress on initial goals
- Address any questions or concerns
- Adjust onboarding plan if needed

### 90-Day Review
Formal review to:
- Assess performance against expectations
- Provide detailed feedback
- Confirm successful completion of probationary period
- Set goals for next quarter

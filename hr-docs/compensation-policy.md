# Compensation Policy

## Purpose

This policy outlines our compensation philosophy, pay structures, and practices to ensure fair, competitive, and equitable compensation for all employees while supporting our business objectives.

## Compensation Philosophy

Our compensation program is designed to:
- Attract and retain top talent
- Reward performance and contributions
- Maintain internal equity and external competitiveness
- Support our company values and culture
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

## Pay Structure

### Job Levels and Grades

Positions are organized into job levels based on:
- Scope of responsibility
- Required skills and experience
- Impact on business results
- Market value

**Job Levels:**
- Individual Contributor: IC1 (Entry) through IC6 (Principal/Fellow)
- Management: M1 (Manager) through M5 (Executive)
- Each level has defined salary ranges based on market data

### Salary Ranges

Each position has a salary range with three zones:
- **Minimum:** Entry point for the role
- **Midpoint:** Fully competent performance
- **Maximum:** Exceptional sustained performance

**Range Positioning:**
- New hires typically start between minimum and midpoint
- Progression through range based on performance and experience
- Employees consistently at maximum may be candidates for promotion

## Base Salary

### Starting Salary Determination

Starting salary considers:
- Job level and salary range
- Relevant experience and skills
- Internal equity with current employees
- Market conditions and competition
- Geographic location (if applicable)
- Budget constraints

### Salary Reviews

**Annual Merit Review:**
- Conducted each April
- Based on prior year performance (January-December)
- Merit increases effective May 1st
- Typical merit budget: 3-5% of payroll (varies by company performance)

**Performance-Based Increases:**
- Exceeds Expectations: 4-6% increase
- Meets Expectations: 2-4% increase
- Needs Improvement: 0-2% increase
- Does Not Meet: No increase, performance improvement plan

### Promotional Increases

- Promotions typically include 8-15% salary increase
- Adjustment brings salary to appropriate level in new range
- Effective date aligns with promotion date
- Separate from annual merit cycle

### Market Adjustments

Periodic adjustments made when:
- Market data shows significant gaps
- Retention risk identified
- Internal equity issues arise
- Role responsibilities significantly change

## Variable Compensation

### Annual Bonus Program

**Eligibility:**
- All regular full-time employees
- Employed on December 31st of performance year
- Not on performance improvement plan

**Target Bonus by Level:**
- IC1-IC2: 5% of base salary
- IC3-IC4: 10% of base salary
- IC5-IC6: 15% of base salary
- M1-M2: 15% of base salary
- M3-M4: 20% of base salary
- M5+: 25-40% of base salary

**Payout Determination:**
- Company performance (50% weight)
- Department performance (25% weight)
- Individual performance (25% weight)
- Actual payout: 0-200% of target

**Payment Timing:**
- Paid in March following performance year
- Must be actively employed at time of payment
- Pro-rated for new hires (hired before October 1st)

### Sales Incentive Plans

Sales roles have separate commission/incentive plans:
- Documented in individual sales compensation plans
- Typically higher variable component than base
- Quarterly or monthly payment schedules
- Separate from annual bonus program

### Spot Bonuses

Managers may award spot bonuses for:
- Exceptional project completion
- Going above and beyond
- Critical problem resolution
- Typically $500-$2,500

**Process:**
- Manager submits request to HR with justification
- Department head approval required
- Paid in next payroll cycle
- Not guaranteed or recurring

## Equity Compensation

### Stock Option Program

**Eligibility:**
- IC4 level and above
- Management levels M1 and above
- Key individual contributors (by exception)

**Grant Types:**
- New hire grants: Issued upon hire
- Annual refresh grants: Based on performance
- Promotional grants: Upon promotion to eligible level
- Retention grants: Strategic retention situations

**Vesting Schedule:**
- Standard: 4-year vesting, 25% per year
- Cliff: 1-year cliff before first vesting
- Acceleration: Possible in acquisition scenarios (per plan documents)

**Exercise and Taxation:**
- Refer to stock plan documents for details
- Consult personal tax advisor
- Exercise windows defined in plan

## Pay Practices

### Pay Frequency

- Salaried employees: Bi-weekly (26 pay periods)
- Pay dates: Every other Friday
- Direct deposit required (check by exception only)

### Overtime

**Non-Exempt Employees:**
- Overtime paid at 1.5x regular rate for hours over 40/week
- Must be pre-approved by manager
- Accurately record all hours worked in Workday

**Exempt Employees:**
- Not eligible for overtime pay
- Expected to work hours necessary to complete job duties
- Typically 40-50 hours per week

### Pay Transparency

**What We Share:**
- Salary ranges for posted positions (where required by law)
- General compensation philosophy
- How pay decisions are made

**What We Don't Share:**
- Individual employee salaries
- Specific merit increase amounts for others
- Detailed bonus calculations

**Employee Rights:**
- You may discuss your own compensation
- We don't prohibit pay discussions among employees
- Protected by law in most jurisdictions

## Geographic Pay Differences

### Location-Based Pay

For remote employees, salary may be adjusted based on:
- Local market rates
- Cost of labor in location
- Geographic pay zones (if applicable)

**Relocation:**
- Notify HR of permanent address changes
- Pay may be adjusted if moving to different pay zone
- Changes effective 60 days after relocation

## Special Situations

### Internships and Co-ops

- Paid hourly at competitive rates
- Typically $20-$35/hour based on level and field
- Not eligible for benefits or bonus programs
- May receive housing stipend for relocation

### Temporary and Contract Workers

- Paid per contract terms
- Not eligible for employee benefits
- No bonus or equity participation
- Separate from regular employee compensation programs

### Severance

**Involuntary Termination (without cause):**
- 2 weeks base pay per year of service
- Minimum 4 weeks, maximum 26 weeks
- Continuation of benefits during severance period
- Outplacement services provided

**Not Eligible:**
- Voluntary resignation
- Termination for cause
- Performance-related terminations after PIP

## Pay Equity and Compliance

### Equal Pay Commitment

We are committed to pay equity:
- Regular pay equity audits conducted
- Adjustments made to address unexplained gaps
- Hiring and promotion decisions free from bias
- Transparent pay practices

### Legal Compliance

We comply with all applicable laws:
- Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)
- Equal Pay Act
- State and local pay transparency laws
- Minimum wage requirements
- Overtime regulations

### Salary History

- We do not ask for salary history in hiring process
- Decisions based on role, market, and candidate qualifications
- Compliant with salary history ban laws

## Compensation Reviews and Appeals

### Discussing Your Pay

Employees are encouraged to:
- Discuss compensation with manager during 1-on-1s
- Ask questions about pay decisions
- Understand their position in salary range
- Request feedback on advancement opportunities

### Requesting a Review

If you believe your compensation is not aligned with policy:
1. Discuss with your direct manager first
2. If unresolved, contact HR
3. Provide specific concerns and supporting information
4. HR will review and respond within 30 days

### Appeal Process

Formal appeals may be submitted to:
- HR Director
- Include documentation and rationale
- Review completed within 45 days
- Decision is final

## Payroll Administration

### Payroll Changes

**Effective Dates:**
- Merit increases: May 1st (annual cycle)
- Promotions: Effective date of promotion
- Market adjustments: As approved
- New hires: Start date

**Processing:**
- Changes processed by HR in Workday
- Reflected in next available payroll
- Confirmation sent via Workday notification

### Payroll Deductions

**Standard Deductions:**
- Federal, state, local taxes
- Social Security and Medicare
- Benefits premiums
- 401(k) contributions
- Garnishments (if applicable)

**Voluntary Deductions:**
- Additional retirement contributions
- HSA/FSA contributions
- Charitable giving
- Employee stock purchase plan

### Pay Statements

- Available in Workday on pay date
- Shows gross pay, deductions, net pay
- Year-to-date totals included
- Retain for tax purposes

### Payroll Errors

If you identify an error:
1. Contact Payroll immediately: payroll@company.com
2. Provide pay date and description of error
3. Corrections made in next payroll cycle
4. Underpayments corrected via off-cycle check if significant

## Tax Documents

### W-2 Forms

- Available by January 31st each year
- Accessible in Workday
- Mailed to address on file
- Contact Payroll for corrections

### W-4 Updates

Update tax withholding:
- In Workday under Pay > Tax Withholding
- Changes effective next payroll
- Review annually or after life changes
- Consult tax advisor for guidance

## Retirement Plans

### 401(k) Plan

**Eligibility:** First of month following 30 days of employment

**Employee Contributions:**
- Up to IRS annual limit ($23,000 in 2026)
- Roth and traditional options available
- Change contribution amount anytime

**Company Match:**
- 100% match on first 3% of salary
- 50% match on next 2% of salary
- Maximum company match: 4% of salary
- Vested immediately

**Enrollment:**
- Enroll in Workday
- Select investment options
- Designate beneficiaries

- Base salary

- Health and welfare benefits
- Retirement contributions
- Paid time off

## Confidentiality

Compensation information is confidential:
- Do not share others' compensation details
- Protect sensitive compensation data
- Follow data security protocols
- Report breaches to HR immediately

**Note:** Employees have the legal right to discuss their own compensation with coworkers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will I get a raise?**
A: Annual merit reviews occur in April with increases effective May 1st. Promotional increases happen at time of promotion.

**Q: How is my bonus calculated?**
A: Based on company, department, and individual performance against targets. See Variable Compensation section for details.

**Q: Can I negotiate my salary?**
A: Yes, during the offer stage. After hire, compensation follows standard review cycles unless promotion or significant role change occurs.

**Q: What if I think I'm underpaid?**
A: Discuss with your manager first, then HR if needed. We conduct regular pay equity reviews.

**Q: Are bonuses guaranteed?**
A: No, bonuses are variable and based on performance. Target amounts are not guarantees.

**Q: How do I know my job level?**
A: Your job level is shown in Workday under your job profile. Discuss with your manager if you have questions.

**Q: Can I see the salary range for my position?**
A: Yes, contact HR to understand your position's salary range and where you fall within it.

**Q: What happens to my equity if I leave?**
A: Refer to your stock plan documents. Generally, unvested options are forfeited and vested options must be exercised within 90 days.

# Workday User Guide

## Overview

Workday is our comprehensive Human Capital Management (HCM) system that serves as your central hub for all HR-related activities. This guide will help you navigate the platform and complete common tasks.

## Accessing Workday

**Login URL:** https://workday.company.com
**Username:** Your company email address
**Password:** Set during onboarding (reset via "Forgot Password" link)

**Mobile App:** Download "Workday" from the App Store or Google Play

## Dashboard Overview

When you log in, you'll see your personalized dashboard with:
- Pending tasks and approvals
- Upcoming time off
- Recent pay stubs
- Company announcements
- Quick links to frequently used features

## Common Tasks

### Viewing Your Pay Information

1. Click on the **Pay** application from your dashboard
2. Select **View Payslip** to see current or past pay stubs
3. Use filters to view specific pay periods
4. Download PDF copies for your records

### Requesting Time Off

1. Navigate to **Time Off** from the main menu
2. Click **Request Time Off**
3. Select the time off type (PTO, sick leave, etc.)
4. Choose your dates and enter the number of hours/days
5. Add any comments for your manager
6. Click **Submit** - your manager will receive a notification

**Tip:** Check your time off balance before submitting requests

### Updating Personal Information

1. Go to **Personal Information** from your profile menu
2. Click **Contact Information** to update:
   - Home address
   - Phone numbers
   - Emergency contacts
3. Click **Edit** next to the section you want to change
4. Make your updates and click **Submit**

**Note:** Some changes (like name or tax information) may require HR approval

### Viewing Benefits

1. Click **Benefits** from the main menu
2. View your current enrollments and coverage details
3. Access benefits documents and ID cards
4. Make changes during open enrollment periods

### Performance Management

1. Navigate to **Performance** to access:
   - Goal tracking and updates
   - Performance reviews
   - Feedback requests
   - Development plans
2. Update goals regularly throughout the year
3. Complete self-assessments when prompted

### Learning and Development

1. Go to **Learning** to browse available courses
2. Search by topic, skill, or requirement
3. Enroll in courses and track your progress
4. View your learning history and certifications

### Expense Reports

1. Click **Expenses** from the menu
2. Select **Create Expense Report**
3. Add expenses with receipts (photo upload available)
4. Categorize each expense correctly
5. Submit for approval

**Tip:** Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense

### Updating Direct Deposit

1. Go to **Pay** > **Payment Elections**
2. Click **Direct Deposit**
3. Add or edit bank account information
4. You can split deposits across multiple accounts
5. Changes take effect in the next pay cycle

### Viewing Organization Chart

1. Click on your name/profile
2. Select **View Organization**
3. Navigate through the org chart to see reporting structures
4. Click on any employee to view their profile (if permissions allow)

### Requesting Documents

1. Go to **Personal Information**
2. Select **Documents**
3. Request employment verification letters, tax forms, or other documents
4. Documents are typically available within 2-3 business days

## Manager-Specific Features

### Approving Time Off Requests

1. Check your **Inbox** for pending approvals
2. Review the request details
3. Click **Approve** or **Deny** with optional comments
4. Employee receives automatic notification

### Viewing Team Information

1. Navigate to **Team** from your dashboard
2. View team member profiles, time off calendars, and performance data
3. Access team reports and analytics

### Conducting Performance Reviews

1. Go to **Performance** > **Reviews**
2. Complete evaluations by the deadline
3. Provide ratings and detailed feedback
4. Submit for the next level of approval

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In?
- Verify you're using your company email as username
- Try resetting your password
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Contact IT Support if issues persist

### Missing Pay Stub?
- Check that you're looking at the correct pay period
- Contact Payroll if a pay stub is genuinely missing

### Time Off Request Not Showing?
- Ensure you clicked "Submit" (not just "Save")
- Check your Inbox for any returned requests needing correction

### Changes Not Saving?
- Check for required fields marked with red asterisks
- Ensure you have permission to make that change
- Try a different browser if issues continue

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often is Workday updated?**
A: Workday receives updates twice per year. You'll be notified of any significant changes.

**Q: Can I access Workday from home?**
A: Yes, Workday is accessible from any internet-connected device.

**Q: How long are documents stored in Workday?**
A: Pay stubs and tax documents are available for 7 years. Other documents vary by type.

**Q: Who can see my personal information?**
A: Only HR, your direct manager, and employees with specific permissions can view your information.

**Q: Can I download my data from Workday?**
A: Yes, most reports and documents can be exported to PDF or Excel format.

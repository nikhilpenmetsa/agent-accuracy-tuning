# ACME Corporation Time Off Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

### PTO Accrual
ACME provides a generous PTO policy that combines vacation, sick leave, and personal days.

**Accrual Rates by Tenure:**
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year (1.25 days per month)
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year (1.67 days per month)
- 6-10 years: 25 days per year (2.08 days per month)
- 10+ years: 30 days per year (2.5 days per month)

### PTO Usage Guidelines
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for planned absences
- Manager approval required for all PTO requests
- Maximum carryover: 40 hours to the next calendar year
- PTO cannot be taken during the first 90 days of employment (probationary period)

### Requesting PTO
1. Log into Workday
2. Navigate to Time Off > Request Time Off
3. Select dates and PTO type
4. Submit for manager approval
5. You will receive email confirmation once approved

## Holidays

ACME observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (3rd Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (3rd Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (1st Monday in September)
- Thanksgiving Day (4th Thursday in November)
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (December 24)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- New Year's Eve (December 31)

**Total: 11 paid holidays per year**

## Sick Leave

While sick time is included in PTO, employees may use PTO for illness without advance notice.

### Sick Leave Guidelines
- Notify your manager as soon as possible if you're unable to work due to illness
- For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required
- Sick leave can be used for personal illness or to care for immediate family members

## Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave
- 12 weeks paid leave for birth mothers
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth
- Additional unpaid FMLA leave available (up to 12 weeks)

### Paternity Leave
- 6 weeks paid leave for non-birth parents
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption

### Adoption Leave
- 6 weeks paid leave for adoptive parents
- Must be taken within 12 months of adoption finalization

## Bereavement Leave

ACME provides paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member:
- 5 days for spouse, child, parent, or sibling
- 3 days for grandparent, grandchild, or in-law
- 1 day for aunt, uncle, or cousin

## Jury Duty

Employees called for jury duty will receive full pay for up to 10 days. Provide your manager with the jury summons as soon as possible.

## Military Leave

ACME complies with all federal and state laws regarding military leave. Employees will receive the difference between military pay and regular salary for up to 30 days per year.

## Unpaid Leave

In special circumstances, employees may request unpaid leave. Approval is at management discretion and depends on business needs.

# ACME Corporation Benefits Overview

## Health Insurance

### Medical Insurance
ACME offers three medical plan options through BlueCross BlueShield:

**Plan A - PPO Plus**
- Monthly premium: $150 (employee), $400 (family)
- Deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
- Out-of-pocket max: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
- Company contribution: 80% of premium

**Plan B - PPO Standard**
- Monthly premium: $100 (employee), $300 (family)
- Deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- Out-of-pocket max: $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- Company contribution: 75% of premium

**Plan C - High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP)**
- Monthly premium: $50 (employee), $200 (family)
- Deductible: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
- Out-of-pocket max: $6,000 individual / $12,000 family
- Company contribution: 85% of premium
- HSA eligible with $1,000 annual company contribution

### Dental Insurance
Provided through Delta Dental:
- Monthly premium: $25 (employee), $75 (family)
- Company contribution: 100% for employee, 50% for dependents
- Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person
- Preventive care covered at 100%

### Vision Insurance
Provided through VSP:
- Monthly premium: $10 (employee), $25 (family)
- Company contribution: 100% for employee, 50% for dependents
- Annual eye exam covered
- $150 allowance for frames or contacts

## Retirement Plans

### 401(k) Plan
- Eligibility: After 90 days of employment
- Employee contribution: Up to IRS maximum ($23,000 in 2024)
- Company match: 100% of first 3%, 50% of next 2% (4.5% total)
- Vesting schedule: Immediate vesting for employee contributions, 3-year cliff vesting for company match
- Plan provider: Fidelity Investments

### Roth 401(k) Option
Available with same contribution limits and company match as traditional 401(k).

## Life Insurance

### Basic Life Insurance
- Coverage: 1x annual salary (up to $250,000)
- Company paid at no cost to employee
- Automatic enrollment

### Supplemental Life Insurance
- Employee: Up to 5x annual salary (max $1,000,000)
- Spouse: Up to $250,000
- Child: $10,000 per child
- Employee pays full premium

### Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D)
- Coverage: 1x annual salary (up to $250,000)
- Company paid at no cost to employee

## Disability Insurance

### Short-Term Disability (STD)
- Coverage: 60% of salary for up to 90 days
- Waiting period: 7 days
- Company paid

### Long-Term Disability (LTD)
- Coverage: 60% of salary
- Waiting period: 90 days
- Maximum benefit: $10,000/month
- Company paid

## Additional Benefits

### Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

**Healthcare FSA**
- Annual contribution limit: $3,200
- Use for medical, dental, vision expenses
- $610 carryover allowed

**Dependent Care FSA**
- Annual contribution limit: $5,000
- Use for childcare or elder care expenses
- No carryover

### Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Free confidential counseling (up to 6 sessions per issue)
- Legal and financial consultation
- Work-life resources
- Available 24/7 at 1-800-EAP-HELP

### Wellness Program
- Annual health screening: $200 credit
- Gym membership reimbursement: Up to $50/month
- Wellness challenges with prizes
- On-site fitness center at headquarters

### Commuter Benefits
- Pre-tax deduction for parking and transit
- Monthly limit: $315 for parking, $315 for transit

### Tuition Reimbursement
- Up to $5,250 per year for job-related courses
- Must maintain grade of B or higher
- Requires manager approval
- 2-year service commitment after completion

### Professional Development
- Annual budget: $1,500 per employee
- Use for conferences, certifications, training courses
- Manager approval required

## Enrollment

### New Hire Enrollment
- Enrollment period: First 30 days of employment
- Benefits effective: First day of month following hire date
- Enroll through Workday benefits portal

### Annual Open Enrollment
- Period: November 1-30 each year
- Changes effective: January 1
- Required even if no changes

### Qualifying Life Events
Changes allowed within 30 days of:
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption of child
- Loss of other coverage
- Change in employment status

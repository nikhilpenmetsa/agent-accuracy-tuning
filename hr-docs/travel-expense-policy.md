# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for business travel and expense reimbursement to ensure consistency, cost-effectiveness, and compliance with company standards and tax regulations.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and consultants traveling on company business or incurring business-related expenses.

## Travel Authorization

### Pre-Approval Requirements

All business travel must be approved in advance by your direct manager. For travel exceeding $2,000, additional approval from the department head is required.

**Approval Process:**
1. Submit travel request in Workday at least 2 weeks before departure
2. Include purpose, destination, dates, and estimated costs
3. Await approval before booking any travel arrangements
4. Emergency travel may be approved retroactively with manager justification

### Booking Travel

**Preferred Method:** Use our corporate travel management company (TMC) - TravelCorp
- Phone: 1-800-555-TRIP
- Online: travelcorp.company.com
- Email: support@travelcorp.com

**Benefits of using TMC:**
- Pre-negotiated corporate rates
- Automatic travel policy compliance
- 24/7 emergency support
- Integrated expense reporting
- Travel insurance included

**Self-Booking:** Allowed for simple trips, but must still comply with policy limits

## Air Travel

### Class of Service

- **Domestic flights under 4 hours:** Economy class only
- **Domestic flights over 4 hours:** Premium economy allowed
- **International flights under 6 hours:** Economy or premium economy
- **International flights over 6 hours:** Business class allowed
- **Executive level (VP and above):** Business class for all flights over 3 hours

### Booking Guidelines

- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible for best rates
- Choose the most direct, cost-effective route
- Avoid first-class unless business class is unavailable
- Consider alternative airports if significant savings exist
- Baggage fees for one checked bag are reimbursable

### Upgrades and Loyalty Programs

- Personal frequent flyer miles may be retained
- Upgrades using personal miles/points are allowed
- Company will not reimburse for paid upgrades
- TSA PreCheck/Global Entry fees reimbursed once every 5 years

## Ground Transportation

### Rental Cars

**When Approved:**
- Multiple meetings at different locations
- Destination lacks adequate public transportation
- Group travel where rental is more economical

**Vehicle Class:**
- Standard: Mid-size or compact cars
- Exceptions: SUVs/larger vehicles for groups of 4+ or significant equipment transport

**Insurance:**
- Decline rental car insurance (covered by corporate policy)
- Exception: International travel may require local coverage

**Fuel:**
- Refill tank before returning vehicle
- Fuel receipts are reimbursable

### Ride-Sharing and Taxis

- Uber, Lyft, and taxis are reimbursable for business purposes
- Choose shared/pool options when practical and safe
- Reasonable tips (15-20%) are included
- Keep all receipts

### Personal Vehicle Mileage

**Reimbursement Rate:** $0.67 per mile (updated annually per IRS rate)

**Requirements:**
- Submit mileage log with dates, destinations, and business purpose
- Starting point is your regular workplace or home (whichever is closer)
- Parking fees and tolls are separately reimbursable
- Maintain adequate personal auto insurance

### Public Transportation

- Trains, subways, buses fully reimbursable
- Keep receipts or provide transaction records
- Monthly passes reimbursed if more economical than daily tickets

### Parking

- Airport parking: Use economy lots when available
- Hotel parking: Included in reimbursement
- Meeting location parking: Reimbursable with receipt
- Parking violations/tickets: Not reimbursable

## Lodging

### Hotel Standards

**Nightly Rate Limits (excluding taxes):**
- Tier 1 cities (NYC, SF, LA, Boston, DC, Chicago): $275
- Tier 2 cities (major metros): $200
- Tier 3 cities (other locations): $150
- International: Varies by location (check with manager)

**Booking Guidelines:**
- Use corporate preferred hotels when available (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt)
- Book standard rooms; suites require VP approval
- Stay within reasonable distance of business location
- Extended stays (7+ nights): Consider corporate housing options

### Alternative Lodging

- Airbnb/VRBO allowed if comparable or lower cost than hotels
- Must provide itemized receipt showing nightly rate and fees
- Ensure accommodation is appropriate for business travel

### Hotel Amenities

**Reimbursable:**
- Standard internet access
- Business center usage
- Required parking

**Not Reimbursable:**
- Movie rentals, mini-bar, spa services
- Room service (unless working late, see meal policy)
- Gym fees (if not included)
- Upgrade fees

## Meals and Entertainment

### Meal Allowances

**Daily Limits (per person):**
- Breakfast: $20
- Lunch: $30
- Dinner: $60
- Total daily maximum: $110

**Guidelines:**
- Receipts required for all meals over $25
- Alcohol limited to $25 per day (with meal)
- Tips up to 20% included in meal limit
- Room service allowed only when working past 8 PM

### Business Meals with Clients/Prospects

**Guidelines:**
- Pre-approval required for meals exceeding $150 per person
- Must include business purpose and attendee names
- Itemized receipts required
- Reasonable alcohol allowed (use good judgment)
- Entertainment (shows, sporting events) requires VP approval

**Documentation Required:**
- Date and location
- Business purpose
- Names and companies of all attendees
- Itemized receipt

### Team Meals

- Manager may approve team meals during travel
- Must be reasonable and inclusive of all traveling team members
- Split bills evenly or submit separate receipts

## Other Reimbursable Expenses

### Communication

- International phone plans or roaming fees
- Mobile hotspot fees when hotel WiFi inadequate
- Shipping of business materials

### Business Supplies

- Printing, copying for business purposes
- Office supplies needed during travel
- Conference materials

### Miscellaneous

- Baggage fees (first checked bag)
- Passport/visa fees for business travel
- Required vaccinations
- Travel insurance for high-risk destinations (pre-approved)
- Laundry for trips over 5 consecutive nights

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are explicitly not reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment (movies, mini-bar, spa)
- Childcare or pet care
- Commuting to regular workplace
- Traffic violations, parking tickets
- Personal travel extensions
- Lost or stolen personal items
- Travel insurance (except pre-approved high-risk)
- Gym memberships or day passes
- Haircuts, personal grooming
- Companion travel expenses

## Expense Report Submission

### Timing

- Submit expense reports within 30 days of trip completion
- Outstanding reports over 60 days may be denied
- Final expense reports for departing employees due on last day

### Process

1. Log into Workday
2. Create new expense report
3. Categorize each expense correctly
4. Attach receipts (photo or PDF)
5. Add business purpose for each expense
6. Submit for manager approval

### Receipt Requirements

**Required for:**
- All lodging expenses
- Any single expense over $25
- All airfare and rental cars
- Mileage logs for personal vehicle use

**Not Required for:**
- Expenses under $25 (except lodging)
- Public transportation under $25

### Reimbursement Timing

- Approved expenses paid within 10 business days
- Direct deposit to your bank account on file
- Check your Workday account for payment status

## Corporate Credit Cards

### Eligibility

Corporate cards issued to:
- Employees who travel frequently (6+ trips per year)
- Managers and above
- Employees with regular business expenses

### Usage

- Use for business expenses only
- Personal use is prohibited and may result in disciplinary action
- Pay statement balance monthly
- Report lost/stolen cards immediately

### Reconciliation

- Review transactions weekly
- Submit expense reports monthly
- Attach receipts in Workday
- Unreconciled expenses over 60 days escalated to manager

## International Travel

### Additional Requirements

- Passport must be valid for 6+ months beyond travel dates
- Obtain necessary visas (company will reimburse fees)
- Register travel with corporate security
- Review travel advisories and safety protocols
- Ensure adequate health insurance coverage

### Currency and Exchange

- Use corporate card for foreign transactions when possible
- Keep exchange receipts for cash transactions
- Expenses converted to USD at time of transaction
- Foreign transaction fees are reimbursable

### Per Diem Option

- Some international locations may use per diem instead of actual expenses
- Check with HR for approved per diem rates by country
- Per diem covers meals and incidentals only

## Travel Safety and Security

### Responsibilities

- Register all international travel with corporate security
- Maintain communication with manager during travel
- Follow local laws and customs
- Use hotel safes for valuables
- Avoid high-risk areas
- Report incidents immediately

### Emergency Support

**24/7 Travel Assistance:** 1-800-555-HELP
- Medical emergencies
- Lost passport/documents
- Travel disruptions
- Security concerns

### Travel Insurance

- Included automatically through TMC bookings
- Covers trip cancellation, medical emergencies, evacuation
- Review coverage details before international travel

## Exceptions and Special Circumstances

### Requesting Exceptions

- Submit exception request to manager with justification
- Department head approval required for policy deviations
- Document approval in expense report comments

### Extended Travel

- Trips over 2 weeks: Discuss alternatives with manager
- One personal trip home allowed for trips over 3 weeks
- Temporary housing options for extended assignments

### Combining Business and Personal Travel

- Allowed with proper documentation
- Company pays only for business portion
- Compare cost of business-only trip vs. combined trip
- Company pays lesser amount
- Personal days must use PTO

## Compliance and Auditing

### Audit Process

- Random audits conducted quarterly
- All expense reports subject to review
- Supporting documentation must be retained for 7 years

### Policy Violations

**Consequences may include:**
- Denial of reimbursement
- Repayment of improper expenses
- Loss of corporate card privileges
- Disciplinary action up to termination

### Reporting Concerns

Report suspected policy violations or fraud to:
- Your manager
- HR Department
- Ethics hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS (anonymous)

This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed. Employees will be notified of significant changes via email and Workday announcements.
